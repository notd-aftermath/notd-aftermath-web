Patch details

Gameplay Changes

  • Players can find a drivable Humvee during Chapter 2: Nightstalkers. The vehicle can only be used while there's a driver inside. When there is no driver inside, another player can take control of it. Any hero or mini marine can drive the Humvee. Can be repaired and refueled at the airfield's repair bay.
  • Moved some of the spotlights that appear after powering up the Power Generator during Nightstalkers to more useful locations. The spotlights won't get removed while the map is still dark. Spotlights won't appear if the mission was completed when the darkness was already gone.
  • The following abilities are unavailable during Chapter 2: Nightstalkers until power gets restored or the chapter ends: Fire Mission, FLIR Sector Scan, Illumination Rounds, Orbital Artillery, Request Gunship Support, Satellite Imaging, Spy Satellite, TOT Mission, White Phosphorus Rounds and Transceiver. Reasons for this include: to reinforce the feeling of the team being cut-off and on their own, to make players feel in danger, as intended; realism (long range communications are unavailable during NS); to balance the difficulty now that players have a better way of delivering Energy Cells to the Power Generator; the mission to restore power felt too inconsequential until now.
  • AH-1W Cobra and AH-64D Apache Longbow helicopters now use fuel and ammunition. Those are represented as items in the gunship's inventory. When a player-controlled heli's fuel drops down to 33%, the pilot will say he's running low on fuel. If the player doesn't refuel it by the time it drops down to 25%, it will leave the map. Players can refuel, rearm and repair their helicopters at the airfield's repair bay.
  • The Cobra's Hellfire Rockets use item charges instead of mana. The remaining charges (capped at 3) are shown in the Cobra's inventory. Rockets can be rearmed at the repair bay.
  • Friendly Cobras will no longer use Hellfire Rockets because they are sometimes quite unfriendly.
  • [Infantry Unit Leader] Defensive Stance can be removed by jumping.
  • [Combat Engineer] Discharge and Detonate Communications Array no longer deal damage to Technicians, Parasite Spores, Parasite Mounds and Cocoons.
  • Black Hawk Down NPCs can be rescued not only by standing near the crashed helicopter but also near any of the 3 NPCs.
  • If the boat runs out of fuel, enemies will keep spawning until it gets powered up.
  • The Hold Position ability in the Leadership menu (X-X) will only have an effect on marines, just like the tooltip states.
  • Increased William Pierce's damage by 65%. He no longer deals any cleave damage.
  • Request Pickup can transport the units of other players. The pickup and dropoff destinations are pinged on the minimap so people can see where the helicopter is going and decide if they want to take the ride.
  • Swapped the Prestige requirements of Request Pickup and Eye In The Sky. Request Pickup is now unlocked at 2 Prestige instead of 1. Eye In The Sky is now unlocked at 1 Prestige instead of 2.
  • New perk: Reconnaissance Drone. Unlocked at Prestige 3. The drone is a flying unit used for scouting. It can't attack, has no inventory and no abilities. Using the perk adds a UAS item to the marine's inventory. Using it deploys an X2D drone and replaces the UAS item with a Drone Controller item. Issuing commands to the drone interrupts the marine's current action. The player who is carrying the controller can control the drone. If the item is picked up by another player, they gain control of the drone. It has a 20-minute battery life. Clicking the Drone Controller recalls the drone. When the operator recovers it, the drone and the Drone Controller item once again combine into a UAS item. This pauses the drone's timed life until the player redeploys it.
  • New perk: Thumper. Unlocked at Prestige 6. Adds a long-range grenade launcher to the marine's inventory. The M79 is a classic weapon whose iconic sound earned it nicknames like "Thumper" and "Bloop Tube". Uses HEDP rounds. Can be used by anyone. Can't be carried together with an M320 Grenade Launcher, Benelli or Browning. Slightly worse than the M320 in terms of damage, ammo capacity, cooldown and time till impact, but range is the same as SMAW's. The AoE is the same as DO/IUL's Rifle Grenade. In true NOTDAM fashion, it has no AoE indicator. A reminder that a player can use only 1 perk per game.
  • Added the M79 Thumper to Care Package's item pool for some classes.

Visual & Sound Changes

  • Reverted the new weapon skins due to an issue with the models. The new Navy Corpsman unit model remains because it doesn't have that issue.
  • Changed the sound set of Cobra and Apache helicopters from Gyrocopter to the same sound set as the mini pilot.
  • The camera zoom gets set to a default value at the start of the game. Players can change it with the mouse wheel, as usual. The reason for this change is that a couple years ago Blizzard allowed players to set their default camera distance in the game options. In NOTDAM, due to certain visual effects, if the player has set that distance too high in game options (something that was impossible in the past), the whole map will look completely black until they turn on Night Vision. New players don't know how to do that. If this happens to them, their natural reaction is to assume the map is bugged and leave instantly. This change addresses the issue by making sure the camera starts at a distance suitable for NOTDAM, while still allowing players to change it if they so wish.
  • [Flamethrower] Swapped the places of the MK-2 Fuel Transmission passive and the Fuel Synthesizer active. The active is now on the FT's main menu and its hotkey is changed to E to prevent conflicts.
  • Added extra special effects when grenades are launched into water.

Quality of Life Changes

  • During Chapter 3: Containment, the extraction helicopter's inventory shows how much fuel it has.

Bug Fixes

  • As intended, Cobra and Apache helicopters will now automatically leave the repair bay after they are fully repaired, refueled and rearmed.
  • Fixed a bug where the Cobra's Hellfire Rockets wouldn't cause any tree damage, item damage or a secondary explosion.
  • Day-night and night-day transitions will no longer break the ambient sound.
  • Ambient sounds won't play while the Earth is shown during the intro cinematic, as intended.
  • Lowered the volume of some game sounds while the EM sphere is being released, as intended.
  • If a sound effect is triggered while the same one is already playing, the 2 effects won't cancel each other out.
  • Fixed a bug where a regular civilian would spawn instead of the green civilian if his home was searched too late.
  • Fixed a rare bug where satchels wouldn't detonate when placed on certain terrain.
  • [Forward Observer] White Phosphorus Rounds can no longer destroy Ground Illumination Flares during Chapter 3: Lost Cause and Deathmatch.
  • [Forward Observer] Fixed a bug where the initial hit from White Phosphorus Rounds wouldn't apply an armor debuff.
  • [Designated Marksman] The Monofilament Cartridge slow effect will no longer last a quarter of a second longer than intended.
  • [Designated Marksman] If the DM hit a target with Monofilament Cartridge without being able to see it (e.g. invisible or vision blocked), it will still get slowed after the stun wears off.
  • Blue Golf company units now grant combat experience.
  • Fixed a bug where using certain abilities would grant combat experience.
  • Units can no longer attack items or trees while they are out of ammo, reloading, etc. Attacking trees and items still won't consume ammo, but the unit won't be able to do it if out of ammunition.
  • Any marines that can use TX-1 Ammunition can also apply its debuff, instead of only Delta Operators and Snipers.
  • TX-1 Ammunition's debuff can no longer remove a Golf marine's items once it drops his HP close to 0.
  • XML-9C Shiva, Smoke Grenade, Ground Illumination Flare and SMAW projectiles won't appear to be launched from ground level when used by the MQ-3 Predator.
  • The number of gank spawns won't occasionally be lower than intended.

Other
